Three simple steps.

Everything runs locally in your browser. No account, upload, or server-side message storage is required.

01

Write

Choose the visible cover text and the message you want to hide beneath it.

02

Copy

HiddenText encodes the payload as invisible Unicode Variation Selectors.

03

Reveal

Paste an exact digital copy into the decoder to recover the original message.

Not encryption.

HiddenText conceals information visually, but anyone with a compatible decoder can read it. Screenshots, OCR, retyping, text normalization, or platform sanitization may remove the hidden data.

Install HiddenText

Open your browser menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

Once installed, HiddenText opens in its own window and its core encoder and decoder remain available offline.
